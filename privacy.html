CRT Companion privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 Contact: open an issue on GitHub

The short version

CRT Companion doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you add to the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Reference data. The CRT, plasma, console, and guide data is built into the app. Browsing and searching happen entirely on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Things you add in The Shack. Gear items, photos you attach to them, saved chains, notes, and wantlist entries are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • Photos. When you add a photo to a gear item, you choose it with the iOS photo picker. The app only receives the photo you pick, not access to your library, and it saves a copy on your device with that item.
  • Settings. A few preferences (for example, which list view you last used) are saved on your device.

We don't operate servers for the app, so there's nowhere for your data to go.

Purchases

CRT Companion Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple provides the app only with confirmation that the purchase is valid. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

If you use Share Setup Card, the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice, and it's handled by the app or service you share to.

Links to other websites

Some records include a link to the manual, spec sheet, or source we used. Tapping one opens the page in your web browser. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your device's backup may include the app's local data, under the terms you've set with Apple. The app doesn't use iCloud sync of its own.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

CRT Companion is an independent app and is not affiliated with any hardware maker named in it.
