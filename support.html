CRT Companion support

Need help, or spotted a wrong spec? Open an issue (a free GitHub account is needed). We read everything, and corrections go into the next update.

Getting started

What does CRT Companion do?

It helps you find, judge, and connect retro displays. Look up a CRT or plasma, check a secondhand set before you buy it, and get ranked ways to connect any console to any TV.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All the data and guides are built into the app. You need a connection to buy or restore Pro, load its price, or open a source link (a manual or spec sheet) in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No app account is needed to browse. Apple may ask you to sign in to buy or restore Pro.

Where do I start?

  • Got a console and a TV? Open Connect and tap Connect It.
  • Looking at a set in a shop or listing? Open The Hunt, pick the brand and screen size, then run the checklist.
  • New to all this? Guides › Signal Guide explains RF, composite, S-Video, component, and RGB in plain English.

Connect It

Why does it suggest something other than the cable I have?

It starts with the console's best stock output. Change Output I'll use in Step 1 to the cable you actually own, and the paths update.

It says "No mapped path." What now?

The console and display don't share a signal we know how to connect directly. A scaler to HDMI usually solves it, and the Signal Guide explains the options.

What do Budget, Mid, and Premium mean?

How much the hardware for that path roughly costs. Prices change, so treat them as a guide.

What does the lag figure on each path mean?

The delay the hookup adds between pressing a button and seeing it on screen. "None" means the path adds no processing delay; your TV may still add its own.

Displays and data

Why is my TV missing, or a spec wrong?

The database covers 1,380 CRT models, 358 plasmas, and 180 consoles, compiled from service manuals, official documentation, and community references. Sets we couldn't find a document for are hidden by default: turn on Unverified models in Guides › Settings & About to list them (they carry an "Unverified" badge). To report a missing set or a wrong spec, open an issue with the model and what's wrong; a link to a manual helps.

What do "Grail", "Classic" and "Notable" mean?

Grails (filled star) are the collector top tier: broadcast and studio monitors such as Sony's PVM and BVM lines, plus a few top PC monitors. Classics (outline star) are famous consumer TV lines worth knowing and looking for: Sony XBR and Trinitron WEGA FS/FV, JVC D-Series, and Panasonic Tau. A set is only marked a Classic when its documentation names the line, so a few family members may not carry the star yet. Notable sets are worth knowing about. Most sets are none of these, and plenty of those are still great.

What's a "15kHz" or "multisync" display?

15kHz is the scan rate of a normal TV, which retro consoles were made for. A multisync display handles more than one scan rate. The Glossary in Guides explains this and about 80 other terms.

What does the bolt icon on a plasma mean?

It marks a detailed record with researched inputs, known issues, and buying notes.

The Shack and Pro

What's free?

All the guides, databases, The Hunt, and Connect It. The Shack's free tier includes 1 saved chain and 5 gear items.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited saved chains and gear, the wantlist and grail tracker, and shareable setup cards. It's a one-time purchase: no subscription and no ads.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it here?

Use Restore Purchases in Guides > Settings & About or on the Pro sheet while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If I don't buy Pro, will I lose anything I've added?

No. Anything you've saved stays viewable and editable. The free limits only apply when adding something new.

Where are my photos and gear stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, its data is deleted too, unless it's in a device backup.

Can I move my Shack to a new phone?

Restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings the app's data with it. The app doesn't have its own sync.

Safety

Is it safe to open up a CRT?

Not unless you know how to discharge it. A CRT can hold a dangerous high-voltage charge long after it's unplugged. The app explains what to look for from the outside; leave internal repairs to someone trained.

Does the app explain how to get around HDCP or other copy protection?

No. The HDCP guide explains how copy protection behaves so you can tell why a chain goes black. It doesn't describe ways to bypass it, and laws on this vary by region.
